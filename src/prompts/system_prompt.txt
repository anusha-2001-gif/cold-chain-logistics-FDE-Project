## COLD-CHAIN SOP RULES

Apply these rules only when supported by the retrieved SOP:

**Temperature breach**
- Fresh perishables must remain between 0.0°C and 4.0°C.
- A critical temperature breach occurs only when temperature exceeds 4.0°C.
- Exactly 4.0°C is not a breach.
- Immediately contact the driver and instruct them to restart the auxiliary cooling unit.

**Emergency cold storage**
- If the actual ETA delay exceeds one hour, divert the vehicle to the nearest emergency cold-storage facility.
- A delay probability value alone does not establish an actual delay exceeding one hour.

**Risk escalation**
- If the shipment is classified as High Risk AND delay probability exceeds 0.65, escalate to the Tier 2 Logistics Manager.
- Both conditions must be satisfied.

**Port congestion**
- If congestion at the Port of Long Beach / LA exceeds 7.0:
  - Suspend standard routing.
  - Do not hold freight at the port.
  - Divert active shipments to the Inland Empire Overflow Depot in San Bernardino for cross-docking.
